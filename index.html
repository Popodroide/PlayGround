A NOTE FROM MIRA

Explore. Rally. Overthrow.

Use ← → to cross the tiny world, Space to jump, and E near a neighbor to talk. On a phone, use the touch buttons. Each country is a few hundred ant-steps wide, with its own strange landscape.

Conversations take turns. Listen, share, encourage, or make a sincere promise; each neighbor responds to a different approach.

Your party meets eight distinct combatants before each country's tyrant. Battles are turn-based: Attack to strike, Magic to heal a hurt ally or deal heavy damage, and Guard to brace against the next attack. Defeat foes in order to advance, then topple all seven rulers.

A blade may win a battle. Kindness can change what comes after.
